How to store Alfalfa Sprouts
How to store

Alfalfa Sprouts

Leafy Greens
Best Method recommended method with best effort-to-efficacy tradeoff 3-5 days

Refrigerated

  1. Keep in original ventilated container or transfer to perforated bag
  2. Store in refrigerator
  3. Do not wash until ready to use
  4. Keep away from ethylene producers

Protect your produce

This item is sensitive to ethylene gas, which is released by other produce.

Revival Tips
